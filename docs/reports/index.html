How to read these. Open a report, then open any killed finding: it says which check killed it and why. Open any needs-human finding: the "What to do next" box names the exact flag or evidence that would settle it.
Be careful with the numbers. The first three apps are built to be vulnerable, so their survivors are the bugs they were designed to contain, not new discoveries. Flask and Requests are maintained libraries; most of their Bandit results are test code. We read the kills by hand and fixed two real mistakes this exposed, but this is one scanner and a spot check, not a labelled benchmark. Secret-looking values are masked in the reports.
